What this lab is

HyperHan Lab is an internal, systematic BTCUSDT research project. The objective is to discover whether a trading mechanism can produce positive, repeatable net results after costs. Until that is demonstrated, the archive shows uncertainty and failure instead of presenting a signal product.

What has happened so far

The system now preserves Binance-backed market evidence, funding observations, frozen research specifications, cost assumptions, execution boundaries, and a coordination ledger. Failed families remain visible because the work is also a record of decisions—not only a collection of attractive outcomes.

What can be verified today

The live page can verify current data freshness, recent market observations, persisted audit records, frozen research outputs, and whether execution is enabled. It cannot honestly claim a validated edge while confidence intervals cross zero. That boundary is intentional.

For collaboration or future conversations

This archive is designed to make the work legible to a technical collaborator, reviewer, employer, or investor without exposing private method details. The evidence is the offer: a reproducible process, explicit failure handling, and a system that does not convert an unfinished result into a promise.
