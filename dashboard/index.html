What this lab is

HyperHan Lab is a systematic BTCUSDT research project. Its purpose is to discover whether a mechanism can produce positive, repeatable net results after costs. Until that is demonstrated, the archive shows uncertainty and failure instead of presenting a signal product.

How the work is run

Market observations, frozen specifications, cost assumptions, validation outputs, and audit events are kept separate from execution. A result can be interesting without being promoted. A missing result stays missing.

What can be verified today

The live surfaces report backend-derived market state, freshness, research status, persisted events, and execution boundaries. They do not invent numbers when an endpoint is unavailable.
