# HyperHan Lab — Initial Brand Specification

**Brand:** HyperHan Lab
**Dashboard:** HyperHan Research Console
**Engine:** HyperHan Research Engine
**Vault:** HyperHan Research Vault
**Tagline:** Find Edges. Reject Noise.

## Positioning

HyperHan Lab is a systematic crypto-research laboratory. The visual identity must communicate measurement, reproducibility, skepticism toward noise, and disciplined separation between research and trading execution. The brand must not imply guaranteed returns or live trading approval.

## Visual direction

Use a dark navy/charcoal foundation with cyan data accents, indigo secondary accents, amber for attention states, green for verified positive states, and red for warnings or rejected states.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0B1020` | Application background |
| Surface | `#121A2B` | Panels and cards |
| Primary | `#38BDF8` | Data and active controls |
| Secondary | `#818CF8` | Supporting visual accents |
| Accent | `#F59E0B` | Attention and review states |
| Success | `#22C55E` | Verified/healthy state only |
| Danger | `#EF4444` | Error, rejected, or disabled state |
| Text | `#E5E7EB` | Primary text |

## Mark

The initial mark is a geometric `H` combined with a restrained orbit/trajectory line. It is defined in `assets/branding/hyperhan-lab-mark.svg`. The mark is an identity asset, not a market chart and must never be used to imply a signal or forecast.

## Product rules

Dashboard labels and status must come from the backend/system state. Branding may be static, but market values, signals, confidence, expected move, research outcomes, and availability states may never be placeholders, dummy data, mocks, or fabricated values.

This is the initial approved version and may be revised through a future significant batch with a new commit and ledger reference.
