# HyperHan Lab — Initial Brand Specification

**Brand:** HyperHan Lab
**Dashboard:** HyperHan Research Archive
**Engine:** HyperHan Research Engine
**Vault:** HyperHan Research Vault
**Primary archive statement:** No validated edge.
**Research principle:** Find Edges. Reject Noise.

## Positioning

HyperHan Lab is a systematic crypto-research portfolio and evidence archive. The visual identity must communicate measurement, reproducibility, skepticism toward noise, and disciplined separation between research evidence and trading execution. The brand must not imply guaranteed returns, an active signal, profitability, or live trading approval.

## Visual direction

Use a restrained dark navy/charcoal foundation with cyan data accents and neutral evidence states. Green is reserved for system health or independently verified positive research states; it must not be used to make an observed direction look profitable. Red is reserved for warnings, rejected families, or disabled execution.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0B1020` | Application background |
| Surface | `#121A2B` | Panels and cards |
| Primary | `#38BDF8` | Data and active controls |
| Secondary | `#818CF8` | Supporting visual accents |
| Accent | `#F59E0B` | Attention and review states |
| Success | `#22C55E` | Verified/healthy state only |
| Danger | `#EF4444` | Error, rejected, or disabled state |
| Text | `#E5E7EB` | Primary text |

## Mark

The initial mark is a geometric `H` combined with a restrained orbit/trajectory line. It is defined in `assets/branding/hyperhan-lab-mark.svg`. The mark is an identity asset, not a market chart and must never be used to imply a signal or forecast.

## Product rules

Dashboard labels and status must come from the backend/system state. Branding may be static, but market values, observations, confidence, expected move, research outcomes, and availability states may never be placeholders, dummy data, mocks, or fabricated values. The dashboard is an archive of live evidence, not an AI product page or a trading-signal sales surface.

This is the initial approved version and may be revised through a future significant batch with a new commit and ledger reference.
