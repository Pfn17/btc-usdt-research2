What this is

A small research lab, not a signal-selling platform. The console exposes real backend observations, research evidence, data freshness, and system health. Trading is disabled; an observed bias is not an instruction to trade.

Research method

Observe→Hypothesize→Preregister→Test→Verify→Reject or promote

Operating principles

Cost-adjusted

Fees, spread, slippage, and realistic constraints are part of the evaluation.

Failure is evidence

Killed and inconclusive experiments remain visible instead of being rewritten as success.

Free-tier first

Bounded ingestion, refetchable raw data, versioned research knowledge, and no paid upgrade before revenue.
